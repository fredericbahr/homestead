# copycraft (Buchstabenfeder)

Sina Weichlein's copywriting business, Buchstabenfeder. She writes empathetic conversion copy for small service providers who work with people or animals, helping them get found, understood and booked.

## Language

### Parties

**Auftraggeber**:
The service provider who hires Sina to write their texts.
_Avoid_: Kunde, Klient

**Zielgruppe**:
The people the Auftraggeber's texts should reach: the people who might book the Auftraggeber's Dienstleistung.
_Avoid_: Kunden, Wunschkunden, Leser

**Dienstleistung**:
What the Auftraggeber offers their own Zielgruppe, such as coaching, dance lessons or dog training.
_Avoid_: Angebot (for the Auftraggeber's offering)

**Zielbranche**:
One of the two groups of service providers Buchstabenfeder is positioned for: Wegbegleiter & Herzenslehrer, or Naturliebhaber & Tierversteher.
_Avoid_: Nische, Branche

**Wegbegleiter & Herzenslehrer**:
The Zielbranche of providers who work with people, such as coaches, dance teachers, movement trainers, consultants and therapists.

**Naturliebhaber & Tierversteher**:
The Zielbranche of providers who work with animals or nature, such as dog trainers, animal carers, nature educators and experience providers.

### What Sina offers

**Angebot**:
Sina's copywriting offer, made up of the four Textarten.
_Avoid_: Leistung, Service, Paket

**Textart**:
One of the four kinds of text Sina writes: Website & Onepager, Werbeanzeigen & Social Media, Landingpages & Salespages, or Newsletter & E-Mail-Marketing.
_Avoid_: Leistungsbereich, Kategorie, Format

**Copywriting**:
Sina's craft of writing texts that make the Zielgruppe want to book the Auftraggeber, through personality and trust rather than pushy selling.
_Avoid_: Werbetexten, Texterstellung, Conversion Copywriting (in prose)

**Portfolio**:
A collection of Sina's past texts, shown to prospective Auftraggeber.
_Avoid_: Referenzen, Arbeitsproben

### From first contact to final texts

**Kennenlern-Call**:
The free, non-binding first call in which a prospective Auftraggeber describes who they are and where they need help.
_Avoid_: Wortwechsel-Schnack (outside site copy), Erstgespräch, Gespräch, Call

**Kostenangebot**:
The written quote Sina sends after the Kennenlern-Call. Accepting it creates an Auftrag.
_Avoid_: Angebot (for the quote), Kostenvoranschlag

**Auftrag**:
The binding engagement that starts when the Auftraggeber accepts a Kostenangebot and ends with the delivery of the final texts.
_Avoid_: Projekt, Job

**Realtalk**:
The onboarding conversation at the start of an Auftrag, in which Sina learns about the Auftraggeber, their Zielgruppe and their goals. It is a different thing from the Kennenlern-Call.
_Avoid_: Briefing, Onboarding

**Strategie-Call**:
The call within an Auftrag where Sina presents her Konzept and agrees the direction with the Auftraggeber.
_Avoid_: Konzeptgespräch

**Konzept**:
The agreed direction for the texts of an Auftrag, settled in the Strategie-Call before any writing starts.
_Avoid_: Strategie, Plan

**Erarbeitungsphase**:
The part of an Auftrag where Sina writes or reworks the texts according to the Konzept.
_Avoid_: Schreibphase, Umsetzung

**Feedbackschleife**:
One round of the Auftraggeber's corrections on draft texts. Every Auftrag includes two.
_Avoid_: Korrekturschleife, Revision, Korrekturrunde

**Zusatzschleife**:
A Feedbackschleife beyond the two included in an Auftrag. It is charged separately.
_Avoid_: Extra-Runde, Nachbesserung
