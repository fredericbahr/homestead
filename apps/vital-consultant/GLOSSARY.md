# vital-consultant

Madita Bahr's work as a sport scientist with a background in public health and special education. She teaches, trains and lectures on movement, health promotion and prevention for schools, individuals and universities.

## Language

### Parties

**Auftraggeber**:
The school, university, institution or individual that books Madita.
_Avoid_: Kunde, Klient

**Teilnehmende**:
The people who take part in an Einheit, such as pupils, trainees or students. An individual who books personal training is both Auftraggeber and Teilnehmende.
_Avoid_: Kunde, Klient, Schüler (as a general term)

### Service areas

**Leistungsbereich**:
One of the three service areas Madita offers: Sport- und Sozialunterricht, Gerätetraining und Trainingsberatung, or Vorträge und Workshops.
_Avoid_: Service, Angebot, Leistung

**Sport- und Sozialunterricht**:
The Leistungsbereich for schools with all-day programmes and special-needs schools: Einheiten that build motor skills, fitness and social skills, plus Betreuung.
_Avoid_: Schulangebot, School

**Betreuung**:
Supervising pupils and helping them with homework, as part of Sport- und Sozialunterricht but separate from teaching.
_Avoid_: Hausaufgabenhilfe, Aufsicht

**Gerätetraining und Trainingsberatung**:
The Leistungsbereich covering individually tailored, self-paid training on equipment and training plans, for individuals and groups. It includes rebuilding after injuries, delivered under Madita's qualification in Krankengymnastik am Gerät, and is never billed to a health insurer.
_Avoid_: Training (alone), Personal Training, KGG

**Vorträge und Workshops**:
The Leistungsbereich for universities and professionals: talks and interactive workshops on sport science, health promotion and prevention.
_Avoid_: Seminar, Schulung

### Delivery

**Einheit**:
A single session Madita leads, whether a lesson, a training session, a talk or a workshop. Larger formats such as a Projektwoche are bookings of several Einheiten.
_Avoid_: Sitzung, Termin, Stunde

### Inquiries

**Anfrage**:
A message a prospective Auftraggeber sends through the website to book Madita.
_Avoid_: Kontaktanfrage, Contact request

**Anliegen**:
The topic of an Anfrage: one Leistungsbereich, a general question or something else.
_Avoid_: Concern, Grund der Kontaktaufnahme, Reason for contact
