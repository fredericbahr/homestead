# office-hub

Gabriela Bahr's bookkeeping and office services for small businesses. She takes on the ongoing bookkeeping, payroll and office work that German tax law allows non-tax-advisors to do, and hands the results to the client's tax advisor.

## Language

### Parties

**Mandant**:
A business that has Gabriela do its bookkeeping, payroll or office work.
_Avoid_: Kunde, Auftraggeber, Unternehmen

**Mitarbeiter**:
An employee of a Mandant, whose payroll Gabriela does. Gabriela is never a Mitarbeiter.
_Avoid_: Angestellter, Arbeitnehmer, Personal

**Steuerberater**:
The Mandant's own tax advisor. They keep the Jahresabschluss, the Einnahmen-Überschuss-Rechnung and all tax advice, and work from the data Gabriela prepares.
_Avoid_: Steuerbüro, Kanzlei

### Service areas

**Leistungsbereich**:
One of the three service areas Gabriela offers: Buchführung, Lohn- und Gehaltsabrechnung or Bürodienstleistungen.
_Avoid_: Service, Angebot, Leistung

**Buchführung**:
The Leistungsbereich covering a Mandant's ongoing bookkeeping, up to but not including the Jahresabschluss.
_Avoid_: Buchhaltung, Finanzbuchhaltung, Accounting

**Beleg**:
A document from the Mandant that proves a Geschäftsvorfall, such as an invoice, a receipt or a bank statement.
_Avoid_: Buchhaltungsunterlagen, Dokument

**Geschäftsvorfall**:
A business transaction of the Mandant that has to be recorded in the books.
_Avoid_: Transaktion, Buchungsvorgang

**Buchen**:
Recording a Geschäftsvorfall in the Mandant's books, starting with its Kontierung.
_Avoid_: Erfassen, Verbuchen

**Kontierung**:
Assigning a Beleg to the accounts it will be booked on. It is a step of Buchen, not a service of its own.
_Avoid_: Belegkontierung, Vorkontierung

**Zahlungsabgleich**:
Matching the Mandant's incoming and outgoing payments against open invoices. Gabriela does not make payments herself.
_Avoid_: Zahlungsverkehr

**Monatsabschluss**:
The month-end closing of the Mandant's ongoing books. It produces the BWA.
_Avoid_: Monatsbericht

**BWA**:
The Betriebswirtschaftliche Auswertung: the monthly business report produced from a Monatsabschluss.
_Avoid_: Auswertung (alone), Report

**Lohn- und Gehaltsabrechnung**:
The Leistungsbereich covering the monthly payroll for a Mandant's Mitarbeiter, up to and including the Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung.
_Avoid_: Lohnabrechnung, Payroll, Salary

**Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung**:
The Mandant's payroll-tax return filed with the Finanzamt. It is the furthest Gabriela's payroll work goes.
_Avoid_: Lohnsteueranmeldung, LStA

**Bürodienstleistungen**:
The Leistungsbereich covering a Mandant's administrative work: Digitale Ablage, Fakturierung and Mahnwesen.
_Avoid_: Office-Services, Büroservice

**Digitale Ablage**:
The Mandant's documents and Belege, filed digitally so they meet legal retention rules.
_Avoid_: Archivierung, Dokumentenmanagement, Dokumentenablage

**Fakturierung**:
Issuing invoices in the Mandant's name for the services and products the Mandant sells.
_Avoid_: Rechnungsstellung, Faktuierung

**Mahnwesen**:
Following up on the Mandant's unpaid invoices with payment reminders. It belongs to Bürodienstleistungen.
_Avoid_: Forderungsmanagement, Inkasso

### Out of scope

**Jahresabschluss**:
The Mandant's annual financial statements. Gabriela doesn't prepare them; they stay with the Steuerberater.

**Einnahmen-Überschuss-Rechnung**:
The cash-basis profit statement used by small businesses instead of a Jahresabschluss. Gabriela doesn't prepare it; it stays with the Steuerberater.
_Avoid_: EÜR (in prose)

**Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung**:
The Mandant's monthly or quarterly VAT return. Gabriela doesn't file it; it stays with the Steuerberater.
_Avoid_: UStVA (in prose)

### Inquiries

**Anfrage**:
A message a prospective Mandant sends through the website to start working with Gabriela.
_Avoid_: Kontaktanfrage, Contact request

**Anliegen**:
The topic of an Anfrage: one Leistungsbereich, a general question or something else.
_Avoid_: Concern, Grund der Kontaktaufnahme, Reason for contact
