# Glossary Map

Each app is a separate business with its own vocabulary. Terms are German, as customers see them, and the definitions are in English.

## Contexts

- [office-hub](./apps/office-hub/GLOSSARY.md): Gabriela Bahr's bookkeeping, payroll and office services for small businesses
- [vital-consultant](./apps/vital-consultant/GLOSSARY.md): Madita Bahr's sport, health-promotion and prevention work for schools, individuals and universities
- [copycraft](./apps/copycraft/GLOSSARY.md): Buchstabenfeder, Sina Weichlein's copywriting for small service providers who work with people or animals

## Relationships

The three contexts are independent businesses, so the same word can mean different things in different apps:

- **Auftraggeber** is whoever books Madita in vital-consultant, but the service provider who hires Sina in copycraft.
- **Leistungsbereich** names the three service areas in office-hub and vital-consultant. copycraft uses **Textart** instead.
- **Anfrage** and **Anliegen** mean the same thing in office-hub and vital-consultant, because the two sites share their contact form.
